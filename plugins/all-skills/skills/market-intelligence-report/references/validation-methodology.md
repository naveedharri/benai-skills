# Validation Methodology

## Overview

Every video idea is validated against four dimensions. Each dimension is scored and combined into an overall opportunity score (1-10). Every idea must map to one of the 3 video formats: Big Feature Launch, Bundled Small Updates, or How-To for Business.

## Dimension 1: Search Demand (30% weight)

### How to Assess

1. **YouTube autocomplete:** Type the topic into YouTube search. Do related suggestions appear? More suggestions = more demand.
2. **Google Trends:** Check interest over time. Rising = good. Declining = caution.
3. **Existing video views:** If videos on this topic exist, how many views do they get? High views on similar topics = proven demand.
4. **Forum activity:** Are people asking about this on Reddit, Hacker News, or AI communities?

### Scoring

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| High (8-10) | Multiple autocomplete suggestions, rising Google Trends, existing videos have 50K+ views, active forum discussions |
| Medium (5-7) | Some autocomplete hits, stable trends, existing videos have 10K-50K views, occasional forum mentions |
| Low (1-4) | No autocomplete, flat/declining trends, existing videos under 10K views, no forum activity |

## Dimension 2: Competition (25% weight)

### How to Assess

1. **YouTube search results:** Search the exact topic. How many quality results appear?
2. **Who made them:** Are they from major channels (100K+ subs) or small creators?
3. **Age:** When were the top results published? Old results = opportunity for fresh content.
4. **Quality bar:** Watch the first 30 seconds of top results. Can Ben AI clearly beat it?

### Scoring (inverted — low competition = high score)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| Low competition (8-10) | Few/no quality videos, only small channels, results are 6+ months old |
| Medium competition (5-7) | Some quality videos but from different audience (developer-focused), room to differentiate |
| High competition (1-4) | Multiple quality videos from similar channels targeting similar audience |

## Dimension 3: Trend Direction (20% weight)

### How to Assess

1. **Product releases:** Has the tool recently launched or been updated? Rising.
2. **Google Trends trajectory:** Compare last 90 days to previous 90 days.
3. **Media coverage:** Are tech publications writing about this? Rising.
4. **Shelf life:** Will this content be relevant in 6 months? Evergreen = Stable.

### Scoring

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| Rising (8-10) | Active product development, increasing search interest, media buzz, time-sensitive opportunity |
| Stable (5-7) | Evergreen topic, consistent interest, not time-sensitive but always relevant |
| Declining (1-4) | Tool is losing market share, interest waning, topic feels "done" |

## Dimension 4: Audience Fit (25% weight)

### How to Assess

1. **Accessibility:** Can a non-developer follow this content? No coding required?
2. **Practical value:** Will the viewer be able to DO something after watching?
3. **Business relevance:** Does this help their work or business directly?
4. **Format alignment:** Does it fit one of the 3 video formats?

### Scoring

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| Strong (8-10) | Core audience need, no coding required, immediately actionable, maps cleanly to Big Feature Launch or How-To for Business |
| Moderate (5-7) | Adjacent to audience, some setup needed, useful but not urgent, better as Bundled Small Updates |
| Weak (1-4) | Too technical (developer-only), too theoretical, doesn't fit any of the 3 formats |

## Overall Opportunity Score

```
Score = (Search Demand * 0.30) + (Competition * 0.25) + (Trend Direction * 0.20) + (Audience Fit * 0.25)
```

### Interpretation

| Score | Action |
|-------|--------|
| 8-10 | Strong opportunity — prioritize for briefing |
| 6-7.9 | Good opportunity — include in consideration set |
| 4-5.9 | Marginal — only pursue if nothing better available |
| 1-3.9 | Skip — doesn't meet the bar |
