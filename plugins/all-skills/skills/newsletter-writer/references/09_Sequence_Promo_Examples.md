# Newsletter Examples, Promos, Sequences & Lifecycle Emails (June–July 2026)

Companion file to `08_Newsletter_Examples.md`. These are the harder-selling and automated emails: launch/deadline promos, the welcome sequence, cart abandonment, churn winback, re-engagement, and high-ticket offer emails. Same reading notes apply: `(firstname)` = Kit merge tag, `[a]`/`[b]` = internal review comments, "(Link)" = CTA placeholder.

Voice rules for this category (from Ben's direct feedback on these exact drafts):
- Slightly LESS commanding than typical direct-response copy. The audience is mostly business owners, "they tend to see through that kind of copy and call the bs."
- Hard-selling sequence emails (deadline, cart abandonment) can send from **Oskar** (co-founder); dailies send from Ben.
- Social proof: first names + specific role/context ("Jesse, a real estate developer from Oregon"), never last names. Prefer "hundreds of business operators" over inflated-sounding claims.
- The pitch: it's NOT "learn AI from scratch", the audience has already tried things. It's "we make AI actually useful/valuable in your business", clarity, working systems, real results.
- Don't convince them there's an AI opportunity (they know), convince them WE can make it work for them.
