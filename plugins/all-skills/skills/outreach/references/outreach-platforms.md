# Outreach platforms and enrichment tools: recommendations + pricing

What to run the campaign on, and what it costs. Pull the relevant rows into `cadence.md` so the operator knows the recommended stack and the spend. Prices are USD, last verified June 2026; treat headline plan prices as solid and credit/volume allotments as worth a live-trial confirm (vendors revise those often).

## 1. Quick recommendation by need

| Need | Recommend | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email at volume, tight budget | **Smartlead** ($39 to $94/mo) | Unlimited mailboxes + seats on every tier; cheapest way to connect many inboxes |
| Email + a built-in lead database in one tool | **Instantly** ($47 to $97/mo) | Flat-rate unlimited inboxes plus an optional 450M-contact finder |
| Multichannel: email + LinkedIn + cold call + WhatsApp + task tracking | **Lemlist** ($109/seat Multichannel Expert) | The tier that unlocks LinkedIn automation and the dialer alongside email |
| Find + verify emails | **AnyMailFinder** (default) | Pay only for verified emails; sub-3% bounce |
| Phone / mobile numbers | **Prospeo** (or Apollo) | Strongest mobile finder + LinkedIn-to-email |
| All-in-one DB + sequencing + dialer to start cheap | **Apollo** | Generous free tier, email credits effectively unlimited |

The BenAI default pairing: **Instantly or Smartlead for email**, **Lemlist for LinkedIn / WhatsApp / cold-call tasks and general tracking** (Lemlist can also send email if the operator wants a single tool), **AnyMailFinder for email** and **Prospeo for phone** on the enrichment side.

## 2. Sending platforms

### Instantly.ai
Two stackable products: sending, and a separate B2B Lead Finder database. Sending tiers all include unlimited email accounts, unlimited warmup, unlimited seats; they differ only on caps. Annual billing is ~20% off. 14-day trial, no permanent free plan.

| Plan | Monthly | Active leads | Monthly emails |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Growth | $47 | 1,000 | 5,000 |
| Hypergrowth | $97 | 25,000 | 100,000 |
| Light Speed | $358 | 100,000+ | 500,000+ |

Note: "unlimited inboxes" means no per-inbox fee, but you still buy the mailboxes/domains separately (Google Workspace ~$7.20/user/mo, domains ~$10 to 15/yr). Lead Finder is priced separately (~$47 for ~1,500 verified leads up to custom). Source: instantly.ai/pricing.

### Smartlead.ai
Unlimited sending mailboxes on every tier, no per-inbox fee. Annual ~17% off. 14-day trial, no card.

| Plan | Monthly | Active leads | Monthly sends | Notable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Base | $39 | 2,000 | 6,000 | 1 seat, no API |
| Pro | $94 | 30,000 | 90,000 | API + webhooks, unlimited seats, white-label |
| Unlimited Smart | $174 | Unlimited | 150,000 | premium warmup pool, multi-client |
| Unlimited Prime | $379 | Unlimited | 500,000 | dedicated CSM, SLA, private IPs |

Pro is where most serious users land (unlocks API, CRM, unlimited seats). Two soft spots to confirm in trial: Pro send volume (90k official vs 150k in older reviews) and whether warmup is included vs a ~$59/mo add-on on lower tiers. Source: smartlead.ai/pricing.

### Lemlist
Per seat, annual ~20% off. LinkedIn automation and the dialer unlock at Multichannel Expert, not Email Pro. 14-day full-feature trial; a perpetual free Chrome-extension tier exists (no sending).

| Plan | Monthly/seat | Inboxes/seat | LinkedIn | Cold call | Lead DB |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Email Pro | $79 | 3 | No | No | Yes |
| Multichannel Expert | $109 | 5 | **Yes** | Yes (calls often metered) | Yes |
| Outreach Scale | Custom (min 5 seats) | Unlimited | Yes | Yes | Yes |

For any cadence that touches LinkedIn or phone, Multichannel Expert is the floor. Enrichment credit counts vary by source, confirm in-app. Source: lemlist.com/pricing.

## 3. Enrichment tools

### Apollo.io
Per user; email credits effectively unlimited (fair-use ~250/day) on every tier, mobile + export metered. Annual ~17 to 20% off. Perpetual free plan.

| Plan | Monthly | Mobile credits/mo | Export credits/mo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 5 | 10 |
| Basic | $59 | 75 | 1,000 |
| Professional | $99 | 100 | 2,000 |
| Organization | $149 (min 3 seats) | 200 | 4,000 |

Phone numbers cost ~8x a normal credit. Source: apollo.io/pricing.

### AnyMailFinder (default email finder)
You pay only for verified emails (1 credit per verified result; risky/not-found/duplicate are free; bounce-refund guarantee). Continuous credit slider, reviewers bucket it. Annual ~33% off. Bulk + API + unlimited seats on every tier. 100 free credits to start.

| Tier | Monthly | Verified emails/mo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $14 to $29 | 50 to 400 |
| Standard | $49 to $89 | 1,000 to 2,000 |
| Scale | $149 to $199 | 5,000 to 10,000 |
| Ultimate | $299 to $499 | 25,000 to 50,000 |

Cost per verified email falls from ~$0.07 to 0.18 (Starter) to ~$0.01 (Ultimate). Decision-maker search = 2 credits. Source: anymailfinder.com/pricing.

### Prospeo.io (phone + LinkedIn email)
Email + phone on one shared credit pool, includes a LinkedIn email finder and LinkedIn-to-mobile. Per user, annual ~25% off, no rollover. Free plan ~100 credits/mo, no card.

| Plan | Monthly/user | Credits/mo | Email | Mobile |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $49 | 2,000 | 1 credit | 10 credits |
| Growth | $99 | 5,000 | 1 credit | 10 credits |
| Pro | $249 | 15,000 | 1 credit | 10 credits |

Mobile = 10x an email, so Starter's 2,000 credits is ~2,000 emails OR ~200 mobiles (~$0.04/email, ~$0.39/mobile). Source: prospeo.io/pricing.

## 4. Decision guide

- **High-volume email on a budget -> Smartlead.** $39 to $94/mo, unlimited mailboxes and seats, API + white-label at Pro. The natural pick for agencies juggling many client domains.
- **Email + built-in leads in one tool -> Instantly.** Same flat-rate unlimited-inbox economics with a bit more polish and an optional 450M-contact database stacked onto sending. Good one-tool SDR setup.
- **Multichannel (email + LinkedIn + calling) -> Lemlist.** Not the budget option, but the $109/seat tier is the one that unlocks LinkedIn + dialer alongside email, the right call when a sequence must span channels.

Rule of thumb: budget email -> Smartlead; email + leads -> Instantly; multichannel -> Lemlist. For contact data, AnyMailFinder for verified emails, Prospeo for phone, Apollo when you want database + sequencing + dialer in one.
