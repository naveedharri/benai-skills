## Engagement Phases

### 1. Infrastructure & First Campaigns (Month 1)

Stand up the sending infrastructure and launch the first campaigns against your ICP.

Core Functions:

- Domain setup and warmup: register and warm dedicated sending domains so Northwind's primary domain is never exposed
- List build: source and verify a target list of mid-market shippers, two to fifty million in annual freight spend, decision makers at VP of Supply Chain and Director of Logistics level
- Sequence writing: draft the outbound sequences for your sign-off, with daily send caps per domain to protect reputation

Outcome: live, approved campaigns sending from warmed domains, with the first meetings beginning to land.

---

### 2. Scaling What Works (Month 2)

Double down on the segments and messaging that convert, and grow volume safely.

Core Functions:

- Performance review: identify the segments, sequences, and offers driving replies and booked meetings
- Volume scaling: expand list coverage and add sending capacity within deliverability limits
- Calendar handoff: coordinate with Marcus so qualified meetings route cleanly to the AEs in HubSpot

Outcome: meeting volume scaling toward the 15 to 20 per month target, with the best-performing sequences clear.

---

### 3. Optimization & Handoff (Month 3)

Tune the motion and hand over a documented, repeatable system.

Core Functions:

- Continuous optimization: A/B test subject lines, openers, and offers to lift reply and booking rates
- Documentation: write up the sequences, targeting, domain setup, and daily playbook
- Handoff session: walk Marcus and the SDR team through running and extending the motion

Outcome: a documented outbound playbook Northwind can run and grow without ongoing dependence.
