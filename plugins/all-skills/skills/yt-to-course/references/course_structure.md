## How to Fill This In

For each active course, open it in Circle and list every section with this format:

```
- Section Name | 3 clips | What this section covers in one line
  - Clip 1: Title of the clip
  - Clip 2: Title of the clip
  - Clip 3: Title of the clip
```

Example (made up):

```
- Getting Started | 2 clips | Setting up your environment and first automation
  - Clip 1: Installing N8N and connecting your first app
  - Clip 2: Your first workflow from scratch
- Advanced Patterns | 3 clips | Error handling, branching, and production-grade workflows
  - Clip 1: If/else logic and error handling
  - Clip 2: Parallel execution and merge nodes
  - Clip 3: Deploying to production
```

The more detail here, the smarter the skill gets at placing new videos.
