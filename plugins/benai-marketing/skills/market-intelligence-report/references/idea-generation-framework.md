# Idea Generation Framework

## Method 1: Gap Analysis

Use when research data is available from `/youtube`.

### Process
1. Load `niche-analysis.json` for quantitative data
2. Identify content gaps ranked by opportunity score
3. For each top gap, generate 2-3 specific video ideas
4. **Categorize each idea into one of the 3 video formats**

### Gap Types → Video Ideas

**Topic Gap** (no one covers it):
- "The Complete Guide to [topic]" → How-To for Business or Big Feature Launch
- "[N] Things You Didn't Know About [topic]" → Bundled Small Updates

**Audience Gap** (covered for devs, not professionals):
- "How to [dev concept] — Without Writing Code" → How-To for Business
- "[Dev tool] for Business Owners: What You Actually Need to Know" → How-To for Business

**Quality Gap** (covered poorly):
- "The REAL Way to [topic] (Most Tutorials Get This Wrong)" → How-To for Business
- "[Topic] — The Complete 2026 Guide" → Big Feature Launch

**Format Gap** (no one uses this format):
- "[Tool A] vs [Tool B]: Which Should You Actually Use?" → Bundled Small Updates
- "How We Use [Tool] to [Business Outcome]" → How-To for Business

## Method 2: Trend Riding

Use when a relevant tool or feature has recently launched or been updated.

### Signals to Monitor
- Anthropic blog/changelog for Claude Cowork updates
- OpenAI announcements for comparable professional tools
- AI industry news that affects professionals (not just developers)
- Viral AI videos/tweets that signal mainstream interest

### Process
1. Identify the update/trend
2. Assess: does this affect non-developer professionals?
3. If major feature: **Big Feature Launch** video within 2-3 days
4. If multiple small updates: accumulate for a **Bundled Small Updates** video
5. If business use case emerges: **How-To for Business** follow-up 1-2 weeks later

### Speed Targets
- **Big Feature Launch:** 2-3 days after announcement
- **All formats:** 15-20 minutes

## Method 3: Format Innovation

Use when topics are well-covered but formats are stale.

### Format Ideas
- **Mega-compilation:** Combine 3-5 related tips into a Bundled Small Updates video
- **Challenge Format:** "Can I [ambitious task] Using Only Claude Cowork?" → How-To for Business
- **Real Work Session:** Film an actual work session solving a real business problem → How-To for Business
- **Speed Run:** "Build [complete system] in [time limit]" → How-To for Business

## Method 4: Audience Needs

Use as ongoing background research.

### Where to Find Audience Questions
- YouTube comments on Ben AI videos and competitor videos
- Reddit: r/ClaudeAI, r/artificial, r/smallbusiness, r/freelance
- Ben AI community discussions
- Google autocomplete for tool-related queries
- "People also ask" boxes in Google search results

### Turning Questions into Ideas
- Direct how-to: "How do I connect [tool] to [service]?" → How-To for Business
- Problem-solving: "I can't figure out [feature]" → How-To for Business
- Comparison: "Should I use [A] or [B]?" → Bundled Small Updates
- New feature question: "What does [new feature] do?" → Big Feature Launch

## Idea Quality Checklist

Before including an idea in the output, verify:

- [ ] Serves non-developer professionals (not devs, not complete beginners)
- [ ] Can be practically demonstrated with screen-share
- [ ] Has a clear value proposition (viewer knows what they'll learn)
- [ ] Fits within 15-20 minutes
- [ ] Maps to one of the 3 video formats (Big Feature Launch, Bundled Small Updates, How-To for Business)
- [ ] Could include a CTA asset (template, skill, workflow, plugin)
- [ ] Working title is specific and descriptive (not vague)
- [ ] Anchored to Claude Cowork (or a comparable professional AI tool)
