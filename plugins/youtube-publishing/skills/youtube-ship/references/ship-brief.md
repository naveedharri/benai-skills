# The Ship Brief

One approval, collected before any stage runs. Everything here is a judgment call that a
person has to make. Everything not here is mechanical and runs without stopping.

Ask all of it in one go. Do not drip the questions out across the run.

## The questions

**1. The video.** Which video, and its YouTube URL.

Then verify, before accepting it: `author` is `Ben AI`, `channelId` is
`UC3KK7ENB_ierAXvrxVNnbZQ`, and the video is not already public. Say the title and runtime
back so the operator can confirm you have the right cut.

**2. Titles and thumbnails for the A/B test.** Read the candidates off the Notion card and show
them.

**The test takes up to three, not two.** Corrected 2026-08-26: YouTube's title-and-thumbnail
test accepts three variants and the team uses all three, which is why the cards carry three
variations. An earlier version of this document said two, and that was simply wrong.

Ask for the order, because it is set deliberately rather than being arbitrary, and pair each
thumbnail with its matching title off the card. If the operator has already uploaded them in
Studio, say so and treat stage 1's title step as a confirmation instead of an upload.

**The title is needed even when the thumbnails are not ready.** The five-letter UTM code derives
from the title, so stage 2 is blocked without it. If the thumbnails are still being made, put
the title in anyway and carry on; come back for the A/B test later.

**3. The referenced-video (📺) decision.** What does Ben point at on camera, and what is the
URL. Two rules:

- Never carry the previous video's list forward silently.
- If Ben only says something vague like "check out the video above" and nobody names one,
  leave the block out. Do not guess. A wrong link ships to every viewer.

**4. Lead magnet: yes or no.**

If yes, also get the topic word for the Kit page. The slug must be exactly `<code>-free`.

If no, the answer is not finished. The description template hardcodes a "Get the free setup
guide" line pointing at `benai.kit.com/<code>-free`. With no lead magnet that page never
gets built, so ask the follow-up in the same breath:

> No lead magnet means the free link has nowhere to point. Cut the "Get the free setup
> guide" block from the description and import five links instead of six, or turn the lead
> magnet back on?

This question was added 2026-08-25 after it surfaced mid-run and blocked the Bitly import.

**5. Ad suitability.** Confirm the answer set before stage 1 reaches it, because the rating
is permanent. Ben's standing answer for a normal tutorial is **None of the above**, then
Submit rating. Anything unusual in the video (strong language, a firearms mention, a medical
claim) gets raised here, not discovered at the wizard.

## What is deliberately not in the Brief

- Chapters. They cannot exist until the video is Unlisted and captioned, which is stage 1
  and stage 4. The operator reviews chapters when stage 4 produces them.
- Tags. Same reason, plus tags are derived, not chosen.

The old version of this document asked for "draft chapters" in the Brief. That was
circular, and it is why the chain used to deadlock.
