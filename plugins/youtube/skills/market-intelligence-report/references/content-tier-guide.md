# Video Format Guide

Three video formats. Every video is 15-20 minutes, practical, screen-share heavy, and anchored to Claude Cowork.

## Format 1: Big Feature Launch

**What:** Claude (or a comparable professional AI tool) drops a major feature → deep dive tutorial showing what it does, why it matters, and how to use it.

**When to produce:**
- Anthropic announces a significant Cowork feature or update
- A competing tool launches something professionals need to know about

**Characteristics:**
- 15-20 minutes
- Fast turnaround: published within 2-3 days of launch
- Complete walkthrough — not just a reaction or overview
- Better and more thorough than anyone who rushed to be first
- Goal: become the channel viewers check when Cowork updates drop

**Examples:**
- "Claude Cowork Just Launched Custom Interfaces — Full Walkthrough"
- "Private Plugin Marketplaces — Everything You Need to Know"
- "New Claude Cowork Features: [Month] [Year] Deep Dive"

**CTA potential:** Quick-start template or setup guide for the new feature.

## Format 2: Bundled Small Updates

**What:** Multiple minor updates, tips, or lesser-known features combined into a single video. Batch content that individually wouldn't warrant a full video.

**When to produce:**
- Several small updates have accumulated over 1-2 weeks
- There are useful features or tips that don't justify a standalone video
- Fill weeks when there's no major feature launch

**Characteristics:**
- 15-20 minutes
- Covers 3-5 smaller items in one video
- Each item gets a practical demo, not just a mention
- Structured with clear segments (numbered or chaptered)

**Examples:**
- "5 Claude Cowork Features You're Not Using (But Should Be)"
- "Everything New in Claude Cowork This Week"
- "Claude Cowork Tips That Will Change How You Work"

**CTA potential:** A bundled template pack or workflow collection covering all items shown.

## Format 3: How-To for Business

**What:** Sales, marketing, operations, or other business use cases demonstrated with Claude Cowork. Conversion-focused — shows the viewer how to solve a real business problem.

**When to produce:**
- Ongoing, repeatable format (1-2/week)
- Whenever there's a compelling business use case to demonstrate

**Characteristics:**
- 15-20 minutes
- Real-world business problem → Claude Cowork solution
- Shows end-to-end workflow, not just the tool
- Speaks to business outcomes (time saved, revenue generated, problems eliminated)
- The viewer can replicate what's shown immediately

**Examples:**
- "How to Create 300 Content Pieces in 9 Min with Claude Cowork"
- "Automate Your Entire Client Onboarding with Claude Cowork"
- "How I Use Claude Cowork to Run My Marketing"
- "Build a Sales Pipeline Assistant in Claude Cowork (No Code)"

**CTA potential:** The actual workflow, template, or skill demonstrated in the video.
