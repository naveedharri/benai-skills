# Writing Frameworks

The structural skeletons for a post. Present all six to the user in Step 3. For each, write
3-5 bullet points describing what each section of the post would cover for this specific topic
and outcome. Do NOT write the post, show the skeleton so the user can pick the structure.

Recommend one of the top three by default, based on the outcome chosen in Step 2.

## The six frameworks

The first three are the highest-performing frameworks from pattern analysis (recommend these
by default). The last three are additional options for variety.

**1. Build Log / Case Study** (Highest overall engagement)
Best for: Posts where you built something tangible and want to walk through the process.
Structure: Achievement hook, myth-busting (what most people think vs reality), "here's how I did it" with arrow bullet steps, the result (concrete outcome), CTA.
Default hook template: **8.1 The Build Demo**, `I built [specific system/tool] with [tech stack] in [short timeframe] 👇`

**2. Product Showcase** (Highest likes)
Best for: Posts showcasing a finished product, tool, or system with a feature-list format. Checklist style (✅) lets readers scan value fast and tap like without deep reading.
Structure: Achievement hook, brief context on why this matters, feature checklist with ✅ emojis, demo/tutorial link, direct link CTA.
Default hook template: **8.1 The Build Demo**, `I built [specific system/tool] with [tech stack] in [short timeframe] 👇`

**3. PAS + Numbered Playbook** (Highest comments)
Best for: Posts where the audience has a clear pain point. Stat hook creates tension, numbered playbook delivers value, comment trigger converts readers.
Structure: Contrarian stat hook, pain points of current approach, numbered steps (1-5) with arrow bullets, truth statement / insight, CTA.
Default hook template: **8.4 Stat-Based Industry Challenge**, `[X]% of [target group] still [common mistake]. The other [Y]% [do the smart thing].`

**4. AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action)**
Best for: Posts that need to build momentum from curiosity to action. Works well for announcing something, sharing a discovery, or when you want the reader to take a specific step.
Default hook template: **3.5 I Just Knew**, `I knew [dramatic outcome would occur] the moment [trigger event].`

**5. CPF (Context, Problem, Framework)**
Best for: Posts where you need to set the scene first. Works when the topic requires background or when the problem only makes sense in a specific context. Good for educational, nuanced posts.
Default hook template: **2.14 Transformative Insight**, `It took me [long time period] to realise [realisation]. [Transformative insight].`

**6. BAB (Before, After, Bridge)**
Best for: Transformation stories. Paint where the reader is now, show them where they could be, then bridge the gap. Works for personal stories and case studies.
Default hook template: **3.3 Never Again**, `I'm done with [traditional practice]. Every time I [engage in it], [negative outcome].`
