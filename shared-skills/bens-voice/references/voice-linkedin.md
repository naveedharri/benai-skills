**Two hook families, roughly 50/50:**
- **Contrarian one-liner** (1-5): short declarative that negates something the reader believes. No numbers needed. Never softened ("I think" never appears in a hook).
- **Credential + specificity** (6-9): first person, past tense, at least one concrete number (100+, 8, 5, 4-step, 6 months, under an hour). These are the ones that carry 👇.

What makes them work: they are complete thoughts on line one, always under ~20 words per sentence, and either negate a belief or quantify an effort. Zero clickbait withholding ("you won't believe...") - the claim itself is the bait.

## 3. Line-break rhythm

- **1 sentence per line is the default.** Occasionally 2 short sentences share a line (hooks especially: posts 5, 6, 7).
- **1 line per paragraph block** is the dominant pattern: line, blank line, line, blank line. Multi-line paragraphs are rare and max out at ~2-3 sentences run together (the "This is what most people do..." block in the CRM post is the longest single paragraph).
- **Bullet blocks are the only consecutive-line runs:** 4-8 list lines with no blanks between them.
- **Short punch lines** (≤5 words) appear mid-or-late post as beat drops, never as openers: `That's basically it.` / `The tools will keep changing.` / `Your context is the moat.` / `Here's how it works:` / `Here's what's on it:`
- Occasional **double blank line** before a section shift (posts 3 and 6) - likely accidental, but present in the raw text.
- Fragments used deliberately: `Not because we automated data entry.` / `But because we stopped treating the CRM as the interaction layer.` - a sentence split across two paragraph blocks for rhythm.

## 7. Numbers and proof

Specifics appear in nearly every post, usually small and concrete rather than grandiose. Verbatim examples:

- Effort/scale: `I tested 100+ Claude skills over 6 months. I use 8 of them` / `5-6 files do 80% of the work` / `the 91,000+ skill ecosystem`
- Deflating fake progress: `You made data entry 15% faster. You didn't actually remove it.`
- Growth arc: `3 months ago, my second brain had maybe 40 docs. Today it has thousands.`
- Results with honest tildes: `it cut our design time by ~80%` / `take ~80% less time to produce`
- Time promises: `in under an hour` / `live in minutes`
- Market numbers: `n8n's valuation doubled to $5.2B the same day` / `99% of businesses are still never going to open Claude Code`
- Ranges instead of fake precision: `20-30 questions`, `10-15 questions`, `6-7 context files`, `1-2 SAP customers`

**Pattern:** he uses `~` for estimates, ranges (X-Y) for soft numbers, and never revenue claims or follower counts. Proof is effort-based (tested, built, helped dozens) not outcome-bragging.
