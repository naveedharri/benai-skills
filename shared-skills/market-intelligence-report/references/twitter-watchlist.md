## Scoring Criteria

Every handle scored on three dimensions (1-5 each). **Priority Score = R + L + S** (max 15).

- **R (Relevance):** How relevant to the ICP of the business being scanned, as stated in its `Context/icp/`. 5 = directly covers that ICP's use cases. 1 = off-topic for them.
- **L (Legitimacy):** Proven, consistent, real identity, original content. 5 = verified identity, consistent poster, high engagement rate. 1 = anonymous, one-hit, possible bot.
- **S (Signal Value):** How likely to break news, share insider info, or surface trends before mainstream. 5 = insider/leaker/first-to-know. 1 = just reshares others.

## Batching Methodology

| Scan Type | Handles | Method | maxItems | Filter | When |
|-----------|---------|--------|----------|--------|------|
| **P1 batch** | ~30 handles | `twitterHandles` array | 50 | None (see everything) | Every scan |
| **P2 batch** | ~69 handles | `twitterHandles` array | 50 | `minimumFavorites: 10` | Every scan |
| **P3 batches** | ~120 handles | `twitterHandles` array split into 2-3 batches | 30/batch | `minimumFavorites: 50` | Every scan |
| **P4** | ~93 handles | `twitterHandles` array | 30 | `minimumFavorites: 100` | Every scan |
| **Keyword scan** | N/A | `searchTerms` with ecosystem keywords | 30 | Varies by workflow | Every scan |

### Date filtering

All scans use a strict **48-hour maximum lookback window**. Always use the `start` parameter (ISO 8601) set to `now - 48h`. Never look at content older than 48 hours.

### Deduplication

Cross-reference results across all batches. Deduplicate by tweet ID. Rank by total engagement (likes + retweets + bookmarks). Flag any tweet with 500+ total engagement as a potential video signal.

### How to use this list

**All tiers are scanned every time the skill runs.** There is no deferred cadence — when a scan runs, it checks ALL handles across P1, P2, P3, and P4. The tiers determine the engagement filter applied, not when they are scanned:

- **P1:** ~30 handles. No engagement filter — see everything.
- **P2:** ~69 handles. Filter: `minimumFavorites: 10`.
- **P3:** ~120 handles. Filter: `minimumFavorites: 50`.
- **P4:** ~93 handles. Filter: `minimumFavorites: 100`.
- **Refresh cadence:** Re-sync quarterly or when major ecosystem shifts occur. Handles go dormant fast. Any P4 handle inactive for 60+ days should be dropped.
